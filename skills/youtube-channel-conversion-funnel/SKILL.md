# YouTube Channel Conversion Funnel (EXP-002)

## Overview
Synthesized from Enso Lab's empirical benchmark (*YouTube Channels: 38% lead conversion in 60 days*, filed Aug 30, 2026). Optimizes video surface architecture for direct lead capture.

## Protocol Steps
1. **Chapter Hook Architecture:** Label chapters with specific problem/solution terminology rather than generic timeline markers.
2. **Community Tab & Description Linking:** Place high-intent CTAs at the top of the description before the fold, reinforced by interactive Community tab polls and pinned comments.
