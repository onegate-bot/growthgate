# WhatsApp In-Chat Catalog Funnel (`whatsapp-in-chat-catalog-funnel`)

## Overview
Empirical experiment EXP-001 (tested across WhatsApp channels, broadcast lists, and click-to-chat links) demonstrated a **21% inquiry rate in 4 weeks** by presenting product catalogs and structured checkout/inquiry flows directly inside WhatsApp conversations.

## Tactical Architecture

### 1. Zero-Friction Click-to-Chat Routing
- Route high-intent paid and organic traffic directly into specialized WhatsApp business entry points with pre-filled context parameters (`wa.me/<number>?text=...`).
- Eliminate intermediate web landing pages for mobile-heavy campaigns where bounce rates exceed 60%.

### 2. Native Catalog Presentation
- Configure native WhatsApp Business Collections with rich imagery, structured pricing, SKU variants, and direct "Message Business about this product" CTAs.
- Trigger catalog cards automatically upon customer intent signals rather than linking out to external ecommerce pages.

### 3. Asynchronous Multi-Step Lead Qualification
- Employ automated concierge flows that qualify budget, ICP criteria, and timeline in under 3 exchanges before escalating to human reps.
- Maintain persistent cart state and send contextual follow-up reminders within the 24-hour customer service window.

## Empirical Metrics & Benchmarks
- **Inquiry Rate:** 21% average qualified inquiry rate within 4 weeks of catalog rollout.
- **Conversion Velocity:** Reduces drop-off between product view and sales inquiry by removing external browser hops.
