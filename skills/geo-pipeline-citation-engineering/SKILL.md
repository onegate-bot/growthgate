# Multi-Stage GEO Pipeline & Citation Engineering

## Overview
Synthesized from the 2026 comprehensive survey *Optimizing Visibility in Generative Engines* (arXiv:2607.14035). GEO is modeled as a multi-stage pipeline: Engine Search Decision → Retrieval → Context Allocation → Citation & Absorption.

## Key Findings
- Adding concrete statistics, verified quotes, and authoritative domain references raises AI engine citation rates by up to 40%.
- Brand co-occurrence with problem domains correlates 3x more strongly with AI engine visibility than traditional backlink counts.
- Keyword-density stuffing is ineffective and impairs LLM context absorption.

## Protocol Steps
1. **Statistical Fact Anchoring:** Embed extractable data points, benchmarks, and direct quotes in clear markdown tables or structured lists.
2. **Entity-Problem Associative Context:** Ensure brand and solution entities appear in direct syntactic association with target technical problems across authoritative third-party sources.
